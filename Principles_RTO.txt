General Principles Guiding Development of Radiation Therapy Ontology (RTO)

> To follow the guidelines of the Open Biological and Biomedical Ontology (OBO) Foundry (http:/obofoundry.org)
> When possible and appropriate, use classes and properties defined in other OBO-compliant ontologies.
> Use Aristotelian definitions, e.g. A is a B that C's.
